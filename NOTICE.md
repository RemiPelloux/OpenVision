repository contains customization code, verification tools, documentation and
original OpenVision branding. It is not an official product of an upstream
editor or AI provider.

does not itself grant general rights to modify or redistribute this project.

Locally prepared application bundles, executable hosts, native addons and WASM
retain their original ownership, licenses and applicable terms. They are not
included in this repository or its preview release. Dependency packages retain
their own licenses. Small source-contract references in customization code and
investigation reports do not establish ownership of upstream implementations.

See [native runtime and provenance](docs/native-runtime.md) for the specific

has not been reconstructed. This preview does not claim permission to distribute
a completed upstream-derived desktop application.
